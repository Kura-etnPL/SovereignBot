## Selection and scope

The follow-up audit confirmed that Voice persistent settings and Attention's unified inbox/snooze path were already present. The remaining safe local fast-path was Files & Artifacts retention: the existing ArtifactStore already owns unique append-only bytes and lineage, while `published` is a separate publication flag and must not be reused for archive state.

P27 adds one bounded lifecycle slice:

- Files & Artifacts now supports `Active`, `Archived`, and `All` views; Active remains the default.
- Archive and Restore operate on the published artifact family lifecycle metadata. They do not delete or rewrite managed bytes, versions, or lineage.
- The renderer sends and receives only opaque `artifactId` values plus safe artifact projections. Raw paths, cwd, authority, session, and provider data remain outside the public projection.
- Archived artifacts remain safe through direct deep links, source-conversation navigation, History, and restart. Restore reactivates the same family rather than creating a replacement.
- Existing scope/team/channel/coworker/type filters remain bounded and combine with the lifecycle view. No physical Delete, external Share, permission expansion, or new store was added.
