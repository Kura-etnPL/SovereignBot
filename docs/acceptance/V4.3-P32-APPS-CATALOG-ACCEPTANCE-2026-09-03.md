## Audit and slice

The ordinary Apps Catalog entry was already independently reachable and already reused the canonical Connected Apps service, typed preload/IPC surface, local health checks, lifecycle review, and safe public summaries. Search, category/status/project filters, connection/health/installation/capability/approval/cost summaries, and opaque Team/Coworker assignment were present. The highest-value gap was that assigned targets had no product control to remove them.

P32 adds current-scope `Unassign Team / 解除团队` and `Unassign Coworker / 解除同事` actions. They reuse `connectedApps.assign` with `enabled: false`; no new store, transport, URL, token, schema, session, raw path, or authority field was introduced. Assigned labels are rendered with `textContent`, and only opaque IDs cross the renderer boundary.
