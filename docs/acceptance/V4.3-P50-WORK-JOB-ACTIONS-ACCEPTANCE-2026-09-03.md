## Delivered behavior

- Work cards and Job Details use one per-Job action state. Pending actions disable
  the related controls for that Job, duplicate clicks produce one IPC call, failures
  remain visible and retryable, and feedback does not cross Job boundaries.
- Work Retry covers injected IPC failure, visible retry feedback, retry to completed
  through the existing Job authority, and scoped Work Dismiss attention semantics.
- Job Details covers Approve to completed, legal queued Pause to waiting, legal waiting
  Resume to completed, and Dismiss attention to failed with scoped feedback.
- Owner labels use Coworker name/role; workspace labels use the public workspace
  label/kind; Worker cards use safe Worker node/workspace names. Opaque coworker,
  workspace, Worker, provider, session, credential, and raw-path details are not
  rendered.
- Work/Job rendering uses no native `prompt` or `confirm`. JobController, Governor,
  Attention authority, typed IPC, and preload boundaries were not changed.
