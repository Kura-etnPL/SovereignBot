## Delivered behavior

- Attention items are projected from the main-process Job authority with seven bounded categories: login required, secret required, approval required, provider unavailable/signed out, computer takeover, dangerous action, and real blocker.
- Each category receives a fixed safe action set. Retry is available only for retryable provider/real-blocker cases; dangerous, approval, login, secret, and takeover cases retain the existing entry flow plus Snooze/Dismiss as appropriate. The renderer fails closed when the trusted action projection is absent.
- Snooze accepts only 15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, or 1 day. The deadline is stored in the existing Job attention state, survives controller/process recreation, and disappears from the active inbox until due.
- Attention supports category and active/snoozed/all filters. The badge is sourced from the main-process active count, not from renderer-created cards.
- Retry and Dismiss call the existing Job controller operations. Retry remains governed execution through the existing scheduler/pump; no self-approval or Governor bypass is introduced.
- Public Job, Attention, and Job conversation projections redact secret-shaped text, sessions/continuations, provider/backend references, links, and local paths. Operator decision messages are durable but the renderer receives only their bounded safe projection.
- The existing inline conversation Attention strip and Job detail actions also consume the trusted action set; missing actions fail closed.
